📘 PAST SIMPLE vs. PAST CONTINUOUS
past simple acciones terminadas — los hechos
We arrived, checked in, and ate.
past continuous (was/were + -ing) el fondo / acción en progreso
The sun was shining. We were waiting.
juntos (interrupción) acción larga interrumpida por una corta
I was walking when I heard my name.
conectores when + pasado simple (corta)
while + past continuous (larga)
⭐ B2 · EXTRA
Past perfect (pasado anterior): "The train had already left when we got there." · used to / would (hábitos pasados): "We used to travel every summer." / "We would camp by the lake."

③ PRACTICE · elige la respuesta
✏️ PAST SIMPLE or CONTINUOUS?

Acción en progreso / fondo → past continuous. Hecho terminado → past simple.

01We ___ at the gate when they announced the delay. (wait)

02I ___ my passport on the first day. (lose)

03While she ___, it started to rain. (drive)

04They arrived late and ___ the tour. (miss)

05The sun ___ when we left the hotel. (shine)

🎧 AUDIO 1 · THE TRIP TO PERU

Max cuenta su primer día en Perú. Fíjate en qué pasó y cuándo.

⏱ 0:34🗣 Ada + Max

01What happened while they were waiting for their bags?

02What were they doing when the bags were found?

03How was the rest of the trip?

Ada: How was your trip to Peru?

Max: Amazing, but the first day was crazy. We were waiting for our bags when they announced they were lost.

Ada: Oh no! What did you do?

Max: We reported it, and while we were filling out the forms, someone found them.

Ada: Really? Lucky!

Max: I know. After that, everything went perfectly. We hiked, we ate great food. It was the best trip ever.
