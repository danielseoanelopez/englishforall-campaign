① EMAIL STRUCTURE · partes + cortesía
📧 THE 6 PARTS OF AN EMAIL
1 · Subject lineMeeting Request: Q2 Sales Strategy
2 · GreetingDear Mr. Smith, / Dear Hiring Manager,
3 · OpeningI hope you are doing well. I am writing to…
4 · BodyYour main point, in clear paragraphs.
5 · ClosingI look forward to your reply.
6 · Sign-offBest regards, / Sincerely,
📘 POLITE · INDIRECT LANGUAGE

Cuanto más indirecto, más educado. De directo → muy educado:

Send me the report. (directo)› Could you send me the report?› Would you mind sending me the report?

Más frases: "I would appreciate it if you could…" · "Would it be possible to…?" · "Could you kindly…?"

Informal → FormalModales: Can you… → Could you… / Would you be able to…
Contracciones: I'll → I will
🇺🇸 American spellingcolor (no colour) · behavior · organize · center. Fecha: April 5. Y on the weekend.
⭐ B2 STRETCH · voz pasiva (be + past participle)
Enfoca la acción, no la persona (más neutral y diplomático):
"You made a mistake." → "A mistake was made." · "You didn't send it." → "It hasn't been sent yet." · "We approved your request." → "Your request has been approved."
