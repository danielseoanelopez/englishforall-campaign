① VOCAB & GRAMMAR · quién eres + preposiciones
💼 SAY WHO YOU ARE

En una reunión o evento, primero saludas, luego dices tu puesto y qué haces (present simple: I + verb). Sin traductor — practica en voz alta.

Greetings — para conocer gente en el trabajo:

  • Hi, I don't think we've met. I'm…Hola, creo que no nos conocemos. Soy…
  • Nice to meet you. / Good to see you again.Encantado/a. / Qué bueno verte otra vez.
  • How's it going? / How have you been?¿Cómo va todo? / ¿Cómo has estado?
  • What do you do? / What's your role?¿A qué te dedicas? / ¿Cuál es tu puesto?
⭐ B2 STRETCH
Openers más formales: "It's a pleasure to finally meet you in person." · "I've heard so much about you." · "I hope you're doing well."

Job titles & responsibilities — di tu puesto, luego qué haces:

Manager — I manage a team and plan our projects.gerente
Assistant — I help my manager and organize meetings.asistente
Sales Rep — I talk to clients and sell our products.rep. de ventas
Accountant — I take care of the money and the budgets.contador/a
Developer — I build and fix our apps and websites.desarrollador/a
Customer Support — I answer questions and solve problems.soporte
⭐ B2 STRETCH
Puestos senior + verbos: "I oversee strategic planning," "I manage cross-functional teams," "I'm responsible for driving our digital strategy," "My role involves liaising between departments."
📘 PREPOSITIONS · TIME / PLACE / MOVEMENT

Truco: van de lo pequeño/exacto a lo grande/general.

AT = un punto 📍› ON = un día / superficie 📅› IN = un período / espacio 📦
AT · un punto Time: at 3 p.m. · at noon · at night
Place: at the office · at the door
ON · día / superficie Time: on Monday · on May 5 · on the weekend
Place: on the desk · on the second floor
IN · período / espacio Time: in July · in 2026 · in the morning
Place: in a meeting room · in New York
MOVEMENT · ¿a dónde? to the office (hacia)
into the room (hacia adentro)
onto the table (sobre)
🇺🇸 American English: decimos on the weekend (no "at the weekend"), in the morning / afternoon / evening pero at night, y at work / at home (no "in work").

🎧 AUDIO 1 · MEETING AT THE CONFERENCE

Ana y Toni se conocen. Fíjate en los puestos, el tiempo y el lugar.

🎤Speaking: Ask a coworker 2 small-talk questions and answer them yourself.

Ana: Hi, I don't think we've met. I'm Ana. I work at Lumen Design.

Toni: Nice to meet you, Ana. I'm Toni, a marketing specialist at BrightBox.

Ana: Marketing! What do you do there exactly?

Toni: I manage our social media, and I help with campaigns. And you?

Ana: I'm a project coordinator. I work with clients from Europe and the US.

Toni: Nice. Is this your first time at this conference?

Ana: Yes, it is. I arrived on Monday, and the talks start tomorrow at nine.

Toni: Same here. There's a coffee break at eleven. Do you want to continue then?

Ana: Sounds good. See you at the break, by the main entrance.
