① MEETING LANGUAGE · opinar · acordar · discrepar · sugerir
💬 SAY IT WITH TACT

En una reunión, la clave es ser claro pero educado. Aquí tienes las 4 movidas básicas (B1) y sus versiones ⭐ B2.

OpinarI think… · In my opinion… · I feel that… · It seems to me that…
AcordarI agree. · Good point. · That's true. · Exactly. · That makes sense.
Discrepar (con tacto)I see your point, but… · I'm not sure I agree. · Maybe, but have we considered…?
SugerirWhy don't we…? · We could… · How about + -ing? · Maybe we should…
⭐ B2 STRETCH
Opinar: From where I stand… / As far as I'm concerned…  ·  Acordar: I'm inclined to agree. / You make a valid point.  ·  Discrepar: I see where you're coming from, but I have a slightly different perspective.  ·  Sugerir: Would it be worth considering…? / It might be beneficial to…
📘 MODAL VERBS · sonar profesional

Los modales hacen que pedidos y opiniones suenen educados y precisos. Core (B1) en negrita; ⭐ = B2.

Recomendarshould · ⭐ ought to · ⭐ had better
"We should prioritize feedback."
Posibilidadcould · ⭐ might · ⭐ may
"We could explore new suppliers."
Obligaciónmust · have to · need to
"We must meet the deadline."
Prohibiciónmust not · can't · ⭐ may not
"Staff must not share this."
Truco: "You need to finish this today." (directo) → "You should complete this by the end of the day." (profesional).

② THE STORY · la reunión de ventas
★ SALES ARE DOWN

El equipo se reúne porque las ventas bajaron. Practica opinar/acordar/discrepar/sugerir, y luego escucha la reunión y su resumen.

Maria del equipo — propone ideas
Diego discrepa con tacto
③ PRACTICE · elige la respuesta
✏️ WHAT IS IT?

¿Es una opinión, un acuerdo, un desacuerdo o una sugerencia?

01"In my opinion, we should focus on our customers."

02"Absolutely — I couldn't agree more with that."

03"I see your point, but I'm not entirely convinced."

04"How about offering a discount for referrals?"

05"From where I stand, quality should come first."

🎤Speaking: "Your company wants to increase employee satisfaction." Give an opinion, a suggestion, and agree or disagree.

EMPIEZA ASÍIn my opinion, we should ask the team what they want. We could start with flexible hours…
✅ CHOOSE THE MODAL

Elige el modal correcto para que suene profesional.

01We ___ meet the deadline to keep the client's trust. (obligación)

02Employees ___ share confidential information. (prohibición)

03We ___ explore new suppliers to cut costs. (posibilidad)

04You ___ request more resources if you need them. (permiso)

05Which sounds most professional?

④ LISTENING · escucha la reunión
🎧 AUDIO 1 · THE TEAM MEETING

El equipo discute qué hacer con las ventas. Fíjate en las opiniones y sugerencias.

⏱ 0:49🗣 Facilitator + Maria + Diego

01What does Maria suggest first?

02Does Diego fully agree with Maria?

03What do they decide to do first?

🎤Speaking: Do you agree with Maria or Diego? Say why, and add one suggestion.

Facilitator: Sales are down this quarter. What do you think we should do?

Maria: In my opinion, we should focus on our existing customers. We could start a loyalty program.

Diego: I see your point, but I'm not sure that's enough. I think we need new customers too.

Maria: That's a fair point. Maybe we could do both.

Diego: Good idea. How about a small discount for referrals?

Facilitator: I like that. It might be beneficial to test it for one month first.

Maria: Agreed. We must set a clear budget, though.

Diego: Definitely. We shouldn't spend more than we can afford.

🎧 AUDIO 2 · THE RECAP

Un resumen de las decisiones de la reunión. Fíjate en los acuerdos y los próximos pasos.

⏱ 0:31🗣 Jenny

01How many things did they agree on?

02What must they do before they start?

03When will they review the results?

Jenny: Hi team, a quick recap of today's meeting. We agreed on two things. First, we will launch a loyalty program for our current customers. Second, we will test a referral discount for one month. We must set a clear budget before we start, so please send me your numbers by Friday. I think this is a strong plan, but we should review the results in four weeks. Thanks for a great discussion. Talk soon!
