📘 HAVE / HAS · posesivos

he/she/it has

③ PRACTICE · elige la respuesta
✏️ HAVE or HAS?

Recuerda: he/she/it → has. Los demás → have.

01I ___ a big family.

02She ___ two children.

03They ___ a nice house.

04He ___ a good job.

05My brother ___ a car.

🎤Speaking: Habla de tu familia: "I have … . My … is a … ."

✅ CHOOSE THE POSSESSIVE

Elige my / your / his / her / our según el sujeto.

01This is ___ mother. (I)

02Tom is happy. ___ job is great. (he)

03Ana loves ___ family. (she)

04What's ___ name? (you)

05We love ___ city. (we)

④ LISTENING · escucha a Mia y Sam
🎧 AUDIO 1 · FAMILY & JOBS

Mia y Sam hablan de la familia. Fíjate en los trabajos.

⏱ 0:26🗣 Mia + Sam

01How many brothers does Sam have?

02What is Sam's brother's job?

03What does Sam's mother do?

Mia: Do you have a big family?

Sam: Yes, I do. I have two brothers and one sister.

Mia: What does your brother do?

Sam: He is a doctor. My sister is a student.

Mia: Nice! And your parents?

Sam: My mother is a teacher, and my father is a cook.
