INSTRUCTIONS · THE CLOSING GALA

Sunday, eight o'clock. The ballroom is full, Alex is broadcasting live for The Signal, Vera is at table one, and the toast is yours. This is your C2 exit exam, the last one on the map, and you take it in class with your teacher. It has five parts: listening , precision, editing, reading and a required writing task. Then you record a 60-second toast and send it to your teacher.

To graduate: 80% or more in parts A–D + writing OK + recorded toast OK. Your teacher confirms your graduation. No dictionary, no translator.

Your name and your result are sent to your teacher, so he can follow your progress. Nothing else.

PART E · WRITING (required)

Ms. Whitmore still has no voice, so you write for her. Write her thank-you letter to the summit guests (180–220 words), in her voice: formal, warm and exact. Include one hedged claim about what the weekend achieved (It could be argued that… / The evidence suggests…), one image extended over two sentences, and one understatement. Then take the red pen to it: no redundancy, parallel lists, and a short sentence after a long one.

PART F · SPEAKING — YOUR TOAST, RECORDED

After the exam, record a 60-second toast for the closing gala and send it to your teacher on WhatsApp. He marks it pass or redo. Nothing to write here:

  • Thank, one short story from the weekend, a wish, and short last words. (the frame)
  • No script: three key words at most, and nothing read aloud. (spontaneous)
  • One time-buyer and one reformulation, at full speed. (fluency tools)
  • One take, 50 to 70 seconds. Repair mistakes in passing. (no apologies)
