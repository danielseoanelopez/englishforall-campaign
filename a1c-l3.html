✏️ PRESENT SIMPLE

Recuerda: he/she/it → verbo + s (¡ojo con go→goes, watch→watches!).

01She ___ (work) in an office.

02They ___ (study) English.

03He ___ (go) to work by bus.

04My friend ___ (watch) TV at night.

05It ___ (start) at nine.

④ LISTENING · escucha a Nora y Dan
🎧 AUDIO 1 · A NORMAL DAY

Nora y Dan hablan de su rutina. Fíjate en las horas y el transporte.

⏱ 0:33🗣 Nora + Dan

01What time does Dan get up?

02How does Dan go to work?

03Does Nora get up early?

Nora: What time do you get up?

Dan: I get up at seven o'clock. Then I have breakfast.

Nora: Do you go to work by car?

Dan: No, I don't. I go by bus. What about you?

Nora: I work from home. I like it!

Dan: Nice. Do you get up early?

Nora: No, I don't. I get up at nine.

🎧 AUDIO 2 · MY WEEK

Alguien describe su semana. Fíjate en las horas y el fin de semana.

⏱ 0:23🗣 monólogo

01What time does the person get up on weekdays?

02How does the person go to work?

03What does the person do on weekends?

🗣 On weekdays, I get up at six thirty. I have breakfast and go to work by train. I start work at eight and finish at five. In the evening, I cook dinner and watch TV. I go to bed at eleven. On weekends, I don't work. I sleep late and see my friends.
