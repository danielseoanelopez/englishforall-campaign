📘 COMPARATIVES & SUPERLATIVES · quantifiers
cortos: +er / the +est cheap → cheaper / the cheapest
tasty → tastier / the tastiest
largos: more / the most expensive → more expensive / the most expensive
irregulares good → better / the best
bad → worse / the worst

③ PRACTICE · elige la respuesta
✏️ COMPARATIVE or SUPERLATIVE?

Comparar dos → comparativo (+ than). El máximo → superlativo (the …).

01This restaurant is ___ than the other one. (good)

02It's ___ place in town. (cheap)

03The pizza here is ___ than the pasta. (tasty)

04That was ___ meal of the trip. (bad)

05It's ___ restaurant I know. (expensive)

🎤Speaking: Compara 2 restaurantes que conoces y recomienda uno. Usa un comparativo y un superlativo.

✅ QUANTIFIERS

some (+) · any (?/–) · much / many · a few.

01Is there ___ milk for the coffee?

02There are ___ free tables at the back.

03How ___ sugar do you want?

04How ___ people are coming tonight?

05Hurry — we only have ___ minutes before it closes.
