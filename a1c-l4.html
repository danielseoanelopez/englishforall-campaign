📘 there is/are · can · preposiciones
there is / there are There is a bank. (uno)
There are two cafés. (varios)

can (pedido / habilidad) Can I have…? (pedido)
Can you help me?

preposiciones de lugar in (en) · on (sobre/en) · next to (al lado de) · behind (detrás) · near (cerca)
ejemplo The bank is next to the café.

✏️ THERE IS / THERE ARE

Uno → There is. Varios → There are. Preguntas → Is / Are there…?

01___ a café near here.

02___ two banks on this street.

03___ there a hotel near the station?

04___ there any shops here?

05___ a park behind the station.

🎧 AUDIO 1 · AT THE CAFÉ

Un cliente pide en un café. Fíjate en el pedido y el precio.

⏱ 0:28🗣 Waiter + Customer

01What does the customer order?

02How much is it?

03Does the customer want anything else?

Waiter: Hello! Can I help you?

Customer: Yes, can I have a coffee and a sandwich, please?

Waiter: Sure. Anything else?

Customer: No, thank you. How much is it?

Waiter: It's six dollars.

Customer: Here you are.

Waiter: Thank you! Have a nice day.
