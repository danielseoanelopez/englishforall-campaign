📘 PRESENT PERFECT · for/since · already/yet/just
for + período / since + punto I've been here for two days.
It hasn't worked since last night.
already / just I've already checked in.
They've just cleaned it.
yet (?/–) Has the taxi arrived yet?
It hasn't come yet.
present perfect vs. pasado hasn't worked since I arrived (sigue ahora) vs. It broke yesterday (tiempo terminado)

③ PRACTICE · elige la respuesta
✏️ FOR or SINCE?

for + período (two days) · since + punto de inicio (Monday).

01I've been here ___ Tuesday.

02We've waited ___ an hour.

03She's lived in this city ___ 2019.

04They've known each other ___ ten years.

05The Wi-Fi has been down ___ this morning.

🎤Speaking: Reporta 2 problemas de un hotel de forma educada, usando hasn't worked since… y I've already…

✅ PAST SIMPLE or PRESENT PERFECT?

Tiempo terminado → pasado simple. Sigue ahora / reciente → present perfect.

01I ___ yesterday afternoon. (arrive)

02I ___ in this hotel since Monday. (be)

03The heater ___ two hours ago. (break)

04___ yet? (you / check in)

05She ___ the tour. (already / finish)

🎧 AUDIO 1 · AT RECEPTION

Un huésped reporta un problema. Fíjate en el problema y en desde cuándo.

⏱ 0:34🗣 Guest + Reception

01What's the problem, and since when?

02Has the guest tried the control?

03Is the morning taxi booked?

Guest: Hi, I'm in room 204. There's a problem — the heating hasn't worked since last night.

Reception: I'm sorry to hear that. Have you tried the control on the wall?

Guest: Yes, I've already tried it twice. And the room is really cold.

Reception: I understand. I'll send someone up right now.

Guest: Thank you. Also, has my morning taxi been booked yet?

Reception: Yes, it's just been confirmed for seven a.m.
