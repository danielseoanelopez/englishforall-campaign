be going to plan / intención · predicción con evidencia

✏️ FUTURE FORMS

Elige el futuro más natural: arreglo (-ing), plan (going to) o decisión/oferta (will).

01We ___ to Lima on Monday at 9 a.m. (arreglo fijo)

02This bag is heavy. — Don't worry, I ___ it for you. (oferta)

03I've decided: I ___ to drive this year. (intención)

04A: Are you free tonight? B: No, I ___ dinner with Ana. (arreglo)

🎧 AUDIO 1 · THE WEEKEND PLAN

Nao y Ben planean el sábado. Fíjate en el transporte, la hora y el lugar.

⏱ 0:31🗣 Nao + Ben

01How are they traveling on Saturday?

02What time does the train leave?

03Where is the station?

Nao: So, what's the plan for Saturday?

Ben: We're taking the early train to the coast. It leaves at eight.

Nao: Nice! How do we get to the station?

Ben: Go straight down this street, then turn left at the bank. It's next to the museum.

Nao: Got it. I think I'll bring my camera.

Ben: Good idea. It's going to be a beautiful day.
