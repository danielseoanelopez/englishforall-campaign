① GRAMMAR · at / on / in
📘 AT · ON · IN — TIME & PLACE

Las mismas 3 palabritas — at, on, in — sirven para tiempo y para lugar. Truco: van de lo pequeño a lo grande 🔎. Repasa y luego escucha la historia.

AT = un punto 📍⏱› ON = un día / una superficie 📅› IN = un período / un espacio 📦
AT · un punto exacto Time: at 9:00 · at noon · at night
Place: at home · at work · at the door
ON · día / superficie Time: on Monday · on May 5 · on the weekend
Place: on the desk · on the wall · on the floor
IN · período / espacio Time: in July · in 2026 · in the morning
Place: in the room · in the box · in the city
⚠ Watch out at home / at work (no "in home")
in the car pero on the bus / train
at night (no "in the night")

¿Dónde exactamente? — estas van con lugar:

under next to between behind in front of near opposite
🇺🇸 American English: decimos on the weekend / on weekends (no "at the weekend"). También in the morning / afternoon / evening, pero at night.

🎧 AUDIO 1 · WHEN IS THE MEETING?

Sofia y Marcos buscan horas para sus reuniones. Fíjate en at / on / in con el tiempo.

⏱ 1:14🗣 Sofia + Marcos

01When is Marcos free on Monday?

02What time is the Monday meeting?

03When does the big project start?

🎤Speaking: When are your English classes? Use on, at and in.

EMPIEZA ASÍMy English class is on Monday, at six o'clock, in the evening…

Sofia: Hi Marcos! We need to plan our meetings for next week. Are you free on Monday?

Marcos: Hi Sofia! On Monday… yes, but only in the morning. In the afternoon I have a dentist appointment.

Sofia: Okay, in the morning is fine. Can we start at nine o'clock?

Marcos: At nine is a little early for me. I start work at nine thirty. Can we meet at ten?

Sofia: At ten, perfect. And let's have a longer call on Friday, at the end of the week.

Marcos: On Friday, good. But not in the evening, please. I never work in the evening.

Sofia: Don't worry, at noon. We finish before lunch.

Marcos: Great. So, the short meeting is on Monday at ten, and the long call is on Friday at twelve.

Sofia: Exactly. And remember, the big project starts in July, so we are busy in the summer!

Marcos: In July! Okay, I'm ready. See you on Monday.

🎧 AUDIO 2 · WHERE IS MY LAPTOP?

Marcos no encuentra su laptop. Fíjate en on / under / in / next to / behind con el lugar.

⏱ 1:04🗣 Sofia + Marcos

01Where does Sofia say to look first?

02What is on the desk?

03Where is the laptop in the end?

🎤Speaking: Look around your room. Where are three things? Use on, next to, under…

EMPIEZA ASÍMy phone is on the table. My bag is next to the door, and…

Marcos: Sofia, I have a problem! I can't find my laptop. The meeting is in five minutes!

Sofia: Okay, don't panic. Look on your desk. Is it on the desk?

Marcos: No, it isn't on the desk. There are only books on the desk.

Sofia: Is it under the desk, maybe on the floor?

Marcos: No… wait. It isn't under the desk. Let me look in the living room.

Sofia: Is it on the sofa, or next to the TV?

Marcos: It isn't on the sofa. Oh! It's here, on the small table, next to the window. Behind the plant!

Sofia: Behind the plant? Ha! Classic. And where is your phone?

Marcos: My phone is in my pocket. That one, I always have. Okay, I'm at my desk now. Ready!

🎧 AUDIO 3 · LET'S MEET IN PERSON

Sofia visita la ciudad de Marcos y se juntan en un café. Fíjate en el tiempo y en el lugar juntos.

⏱ 1:05🗣 Sofia + Marcos

01When do they meet?

02Where is the café?

03Where does Sofia wait for Marcos?

🎤Speaking: Describe a café or place you like. Where is it? When do you go?

EMPIEZA ASÍMy favorite café is on Sun Street, next to a bookstore. I go on Saturdays, in the afternoon…

Sofia: Marcos, I'm in your city next week! Let's have a coffee in person.

Marcos: Fantastic! When are you free?

Sofia: On Thursday, in the afternoon. Maybe at four o'clock?

Marcos: On Thursday at four, perfect. Do you know a good café?

Sofia: Yes, there's a nice one on Green Street. It's called "The Corner".

Marcos: On Green Street… where exactly?

Sofia: It's next to the bank and in front of the train station. There's a big clock on the wall outside.

Marcos: Oh, I know it! It's between the bank and the bookstore, right?

Sofia: Exactly. So, on Thursday, at four, at "The Corner" café.

Marcos: Got it. I put it in my calendar now. See you there!

Sofia: See you on Thursday! I arrive at three, so I wait for you inside, at a table near the window.
